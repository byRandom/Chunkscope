A bug-fix release.

## Fixed

- **The map no longer draws over the inspector.** Far from the world
  origin, long coordinates made the inspector's content wider than the
  panel, and the map then took that extra width and painted over the panel.
  Chunk and region now have a full-width row each, and any value that still
  does not fit is shortened with "…" (selecting and copying it still gives
  the whole value).
